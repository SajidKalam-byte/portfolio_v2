## What it is

DailyExplainers is a content platform for long-form explainers. The engineering focus is the publishing workflow behind the articles: making it easy to write, organize, schedule, and publish posts that are pleasant to read and structured for search.

[[highlights]]

## Publishing workflow

- **Markdown authoring.** Posts are written in Markdown, keeping content portable and easy to edit.
- **Drafts and scheduling.** Posts move between draft and published states, and can be scheduled to publish later.
- **Tags and series.** Related explainers are grouped by tag and into series, so readers can follow a topic across posts.
- **Cover and Open Graph images.** Each post can carry its own cover image and social-sharing image.

## Reader experience

- **Full-text search** across published posts.
- **Table of contents** for navigating long articles.
- **A Tailwind CSS interface** focused on readable typography.

## SEO-oriented architecture

The platform is structured with search visibility in mind: per-post metadata and Open Graph images mean each article presents itself properly in search results and when shared.

## Performance and operations

Pages are kept fast through caching and performance optimization, and the site runs on its own custom domain.
